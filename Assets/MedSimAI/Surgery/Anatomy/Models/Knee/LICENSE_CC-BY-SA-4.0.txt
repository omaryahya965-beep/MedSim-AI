Modifications by MedSim AI (2026): right-knee structures extracted; posed to 97.5 degrees of flexion;
scaled and fitted to the surgical patient; refined (subdivision, decimation of the shafts); cartilage
rebuilt as a 2 mm layer; muscles sectioned; illustrative resection variants and generic implant shapes
generated with boolean operations; patellar tendon added procedurally. EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY.
