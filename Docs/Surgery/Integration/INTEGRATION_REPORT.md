## Integrated features

- **Scenario selection** (`ScenarioSelect.unity`): Clinical Examination or Surgical Training, Arabic/English, mouse and VR rays. Return to the menu from both simulations (F10 / hold the left menu button). Restart from the pause menu. Each simulation is its own scene with its own session object, so no chest-pain state can reach the surgical session. The surgical proxy rejects `ER_CHEST_PAIN_001` and the clinical proxy rejects the surgical id (tested).
- **AI surgeon** (Dr. Lina Haddad, fictional) and **assistant** (Sami): existing MIT Rocketbox models adapted with scrubs, cap and mask (`SurgicalAttire`). They use the existing speech and lip-sync controller, walk on Person 1's NavMesh to contract anchors, look at the student, go to the instrument table, locate an instrument, and hold one out. The assistant reports a hand-over **only after a verified `tool.grabbed`**.
- **Native voice** through the existing proxy (Realtime understanding, no separate STT; streamed speech; interruption; push-to-talk and hands-free; Arabic/English with persistent switching "احكي عربي").
- **Deterministic supervisor, assessment and bilingual debrief** grounded in recorded events.
